Engineering profile

Architecture without leaving the code behind.

I have built products alone, worked as a senior iOS engineer, and led a mobile team responsible for applications used by Swiss banks. I prefer roles where I can make architectural decisions and still implement the difficult parts myself.

I try to keep codebases understandable when requirements become complicated, especially around security, multiple clients, protocol integrations and long release cycles.

I have also spent a significant part of my career building teams. At CREALOGIX I led engineering interviews during a period of rapid team change, and across my career I have conducted roughly 100 technical interviews.

Hands-on

I do not want architecture to become detached from the code. I usually stay involved in the parts where the design meets platform constraints.

Systems-oriented

I pay particular attention to component boundaries, failure cases and the cost of changing a decision later.

Product-aware

A design also has to work with release schedules, regulation, existing customers and the team that will maintain it.
