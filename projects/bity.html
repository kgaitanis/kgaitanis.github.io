Case Study · Bity Wallet

Native mobile architecture for money, identity and self-custody.

Four years engineering a Swiss crypto and fintech product where mobile architecture had to accommodate real assets, private keys, regulated onboarding, payments and multiple blockchain ecosystems.

A financial product with unusually broad boundaries.

Bity Wallet combined concerns that are often separate products: a self-custody wallet, crypto transactions and exchange, Swiss payment flows, identity verification and support for multiple blockchain ecosystems.

For mobile engineering that meant security-sensitive local state, asynchronous financial workflows, backend coordination, blockchain-specific behavior and regulatory change all had to coexist inside a product users could trust.

Product scope

Self-custody

Wallet creation, recovery and secure handling of sensitive cryptographic material on-device.

Financial flows

Crypto transactions, exchange functionality and Swiss bill-payment experiences involving real assets.

Identity

KYC workflows, document handling and NFC passport scanning inside a regulated onboarding process.

Multi-chain architecture

Supporting different networks and assets without allowing blockchain-specific behavior to dominate the application architecture.

My role

I combined architecture ownership with hands-on iOS engineering. I defined technical direction, designed core systems, implemented production functionality and helped the mobile codebase evolve under financial, security and regulatory constraints.

This is the project that best represents how I prefer to work today: senior enough to own architecture and technical standards, but still close to implementation and delivery.

Product lifecycle

Bity later made a business decision to discontinue its retail wallet and exchange offering. The application that remains available today is therefore not representative of the product built and operated during these four years.

The shutdown was a product/business lifecycle event, not the endpoint of the engineering work. The case study exists precisely to preserve what is otherwise no longer visible by simply downloading the current app.
