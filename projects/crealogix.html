Case Study · CREALOGIX

One mobile platform. Approximately 25 banking applications.

As Mobile Team Lead, I was responsible for a shared mobile banking platform and the apps delivered from it to a broad portfolio of Swiss financial institutions.

The engineering problem was multiplicative.

The challenge was not to build one banking app. It was to evolve a shared mobile system while approximately 25 institutions depended on it — each with its own identity, capabilities, backend landscape and release needs.

At that scale, seemingly local decisions become platform decisions. A feature needed to work for one bank without creating long-term architectural debt for the others. Security, backwards compatibility, configuration, release discipline and client-specific integration all mattered simultaneously.

Representative institutions

The application portfolio included many Swiss cantonal and private banks. Representative institutions included Zürcher Kantonalbank (ZKB), St.Galler Kantonalbank (SGKB), Aargauische Kantonalbank (AKB), Julius Baer and VZ.

My responsibility

Mobile technical leadership

Technical direction, architecture decisions and responsibility for the mobile team's engineering output.

Shared-platform evolution

Keeping common capabilities reusable without turning bank-specific requirements into permanent coupling.

Bank-grade security

Working in a domain where authentication, transaction signing and sensitive customer data define architecture constraints.

Multi-client delivery

Coordinating an application portfolio where different institutions could evolve at different speeds and require different integrations.

What this experience demonstrates

This was architecture under organizational and product scale: long-lived shared code, many stakeholders, real financial institutions and a release surface far larger than a single application. It shaped how I think about platform boundaries, configuration, extensibility and the operational cost of architectural decisions.

Public context

CREALOGIX publicly describes its Mobile Application Platform as a secure iOS/Android platform for banking and wealth management, including authentication and transaction signing. Public company material also documents mobile-platform work with institutions including SGKB, VZ Depotbank, AKB and Julius Baer.
